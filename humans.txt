/* Site */
Purpose: To-Do Boards — a spatial, offline-first PWA. A fixed, bounded page
where any thought becomes a framed, movable, scalable note the instant it is
typed; structure is asserted after capture by where things sit and how big
they are. Built for neurodivergent users: flat, visible, zero learning.
Stack: Vanilla HTML/CSS/JS. No frameworks, no build step, no dependencies,
no backend. All data lives client-side in IndexedDB.
Design: The design system's authority is docs/UIUX.md; every interaction
ruling is numbered and recorded in docs/DECISIONS.md.
