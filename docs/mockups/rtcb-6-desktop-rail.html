Rolling Temporal Calendar Board — desktop: the calendar rail

A rail on the right, a panel when asked

On desktops, unfolded Z Folds, and iPads the calendar lives as a slim rail pinned to the viewport's right edge — always visible, never covering the board. It reads Calendar Board vertically with today's date and a lit dot. Clicking expands it leftward into a panel over a third of the viewport: the full 7-day rolling stack, today lit at the top, the next six days receding beneath it, yesterday already deepest. The boards rail stays left; the board stays center; the calendar is the third column of the room.

Expanded — the 7-day rolling view

  • A third of the viewport, floating over the board's right side; collapse returns the rail. The board is never reflowed.
  • Today is always the top card — the largest, the lit one, the only one with the full aperture ring. Six days follow, each smaller in attention: tomorrow near, then far, the week's tail open water.
  • Yesterday is not on this stack — it rolled off at midnight into the archive. The stack is always the next 7 days starting now.
  • Tap any day to open its full board; tap the collapse arrow to return the rail.
