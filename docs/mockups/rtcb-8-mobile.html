Mobile: the Calendar button & the rolling board

RTCB v2 — mobile entry + the 7-day view
Left — the entry point
  • "Calendar" joins the board-action row, between All boards and Export — the accent-page fill marks it as the one control that opens a place rather than acting on the board.
  • Tapping it opens the calendar board full screen (below).
Right — the 7-day rolling board
  • Today is always the top card — the tallest, the lit one, wearing the aperture ring, its Daily To-Do stretch arrow in the corner. The whole week is visible: today plus the next six days, no scrolling.
  • Scale recedes downward: tomorrow near, then far — attention falls off with distance from now. Each day's lines sit in its own timeline zone; empty days are open water.
  • The midnight roll: yesterday's card folds into the archive and every card rises one slot; today's quickview carries the unfinished to-dos. The stack never scrolls — it rolls.
  • Back gesture returns to the board, per B9. Tap a day to open its full board; tap today's stretch arrow for the to-do focus view.
