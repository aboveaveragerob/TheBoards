The metaphor that actually fits the workflow

Drafting is capture-then-structure. Of the four, this maps closest to what the app does — its trades are about legibility, not concept.

keepsPlacement carries meaning. A trace scrap’s position on the sheet is its assertion — exactly PRD’s “structure asserted after capture, by where things sit.”
keepsCrisp, not fuzzy. Hairlines and flat fills honour §1’s “every pixel earns its place” better than any glow-based skin.
tradesA handwriting face for capture. Caveat is charming but far slower to read than Montserrat Alternates — a real regression for glanceable notes.
tradesShadows & tape are still costume. Trace scraps lift off the sheet and wear tape; §1 keeps notes flat and on the page. The metaphor demands the lift.
