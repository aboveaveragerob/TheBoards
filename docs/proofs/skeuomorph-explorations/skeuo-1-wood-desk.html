What the desk buys — and what it costs

A warm, tactile, unmistakably physical filing system. But warmth is not free against the app’s own law.

keepsStructure still reads. Card, fastener, drawer and blotter each carry a job — capture, place, file — so identity survives even under the costume.
tradesShadows on every note. UIUX §1 says a note is on the page, never floating. Here each card lifts — the metaphor demands it, the law forbids it.
tradesCostume over restraint. Grain, brass and stitching are decoration that can’t “name its job in one sentence” (§1). Peace-by-restraint becomes cosiness-by-detail.
