The one option that keeps the soul

Every other skin fights the app’s dusk-water register. This one runs with it — which is exactly what makes its trade-offs subtle.

keepsThe register survives. Deep water, one lit thing on the deep, dark ground — §1.1’s calm-at-depth is intact, just rendered instead of asserted.
keepsThe note is still brightest. Etched slates glow at the top of the luminance ladder, the note-colour’s job (§2.2) reprised as bioluminescence.
tradesGlow is colour doing structure’s job. §1’s “never colour alone” wants geometry too; here the halo carries meaning a mono viewer can’t read.
tradesBlur & bloom aren’t free. Backdrop-filter and text-shadow soften edges the flat system keeps razor-crisp — atmosphere at the cost of §1’s “every pixel earns its place.”
